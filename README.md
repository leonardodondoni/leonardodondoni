Right now that's **Atlas**, our internal AI assistant (multi-agent, multi-LLM, used by 500+ people), plus the pipelines around it: speech-to-text, call audits, evals and cost tracking.

Before AI I spent 3+ years as a full-stack dev (PHP, Python, JS). Finishing Software Engineering at PUCRS.
